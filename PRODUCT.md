# Puck Haul product guide

The short list of what Puck Haul is, what it is not, and the rules it keeps. `docs/team-handbook.md` has the detail. If this file and the code disagree, trust the code, then fix this file.

## What it is

A free NHL dashboard for fans who want a quick answer on a phone: who plays, when, where to watch, where the standings sit, and what trade just happened.

- Built by Jesse Little of Studio Little. In alpha, so features and data may change.
- No accounts, no ads, no tracking added by the app.
- Listings are for Ottawa, in Eastern time (ET).
- Independent. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the NHL, its teams or any broadcaster.

## What it is not

- Not an official NHL product, and never described as one.
- Not a betting, fantasy or ticketing tool.
- Not a complete trade or injury database. It shows trades that name NHL players, and dated news reports.
- Not a guarantee of what is on TV. Channel numbers and blackouts belong to the viewer's provider.

## Where fans go

Three destinations, always visible, including on phones.

- **Home:** scores, where to watch, injury and lineup news, top standings and the latest trades. The home screen follows the NHL calendar: preseason, regular season, playoffs or offseason.
- **Trades:** the trade wire, with search. Compare any two players, or open a trade to see every named player and pick.
- **Standings:** league, Eastern and Western.

## Rules the product keeps

1. Never show fabricated, sample or guessed data. Missing data is labelled as missing.
2. Failures and stale data are shown, not hidden. Say what happened and what the fan can do.
3. Never guess a channel number. "Out of market" and "Not yet confirmed" stay as they are and do not promise coverage.
4. No usage numbers, rankings or testimonials. None exist, so none are invented.
5. Trade text and news headlines stay in the NHL's wording, with a link to the source.
6. A hockey claim that is not in the repo is checked before it ships.

## Voice

- Plain, quick and confident. A fan is glancing at a phone between periods.
- Canadian English: favourite, colour, "p.m." for times.
- Short sentences and common words. Never hype, puns on every line or vague reassurance.
- Times are Eastern, labelled "ET" or "Eastern Time".

## Legal guardrails

These come from the legal risk report. They are risk flags from a non-lawyer, not legal advice. Ask a Canadian lawyer before anything consequential.

- Do not use "official" for Puck Haul or its labels. Name the source instead: "Source: NHL.com".
- Keep the independence notice visible in the footer.
- NHL and team names, logos, player images and network logos belong to their owners. Use them only to identify the thing shown. Never in the favicon, the share image, promotion or Puck Haul's own branding.
- Stay free and ad-free. Ads, sponsors, paid tiers or affiliate links change the legal picture, so get a licensed data feed and advice first.
- Do not say "we collect nothing". The host logs requests. Say what is stored on the device and what is not used.
- Check with the legal role before any promotion push.

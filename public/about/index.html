About Puck Haul

Last updated November 5, 2026

What it is

Puck Haul is a free NHL dashboard for a quick look on your phone. It shows scores, where to watch in Ottawa, standings, injury and lineup news, and the latest trades. You can also compare players.

It has no accounts and no ads. It is in alpha, so features and data may change. It was built by Jesse Little of Studio Little.

Independent

Puck Haul is an independent fan project. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the NHL, its teams or any broadcaster.

NHL and team names, logos, player images and network logos belong to their owners. They are shown to identify teams, players and broadcasters.

Where the data comes from

  • Scores, standings, playoff series, schedules, player stats and broadcast listings come from the NHL's public data services.
  • Injury and lineup news, and trades, are read from NHL.com. Headlines and trade text keep the NHL's wording, with a link to the source.
  • Trades that name no NHL players, such as picks-only deals, are left out.

What it can get wrong

Data can be late, missing or wrong. When a source is down, Puck Haul says so and may show saved data with the time it was saved. It does not fill gaps with guesses.

Where to watch listings are for Ottawa, in Eastern time (ET). Channel numbers depend on your TV provider, and blackouts are for your provider to confirm. "Out of market" and "Not yet confirmed" are not promises of coverage.

Use Puck Haul at your own risk. Do not rely on it for anything that matters.

Privacy

On your device. Your browser stores two things, and only on your device: your theme (light or dark) and your favourite teams. Puck Haul does not send them anywhere. Clear your browser's site data to remove them.

On our host. Puck Haul is hosted on Vercel. Its servers handle every request and may log details such as your IP address and browser type. Puck Haul does not collect or analyse that information. Vercel keeps it under its own privacy policy, and it may be processed outside Canada. It may be viewed if needed to fix a problem or stop abuse.

On the NHL's servers. Team logos and player photos load from the NHL's own servers, so your browser sends those requests to the NHL. They fall under the NHL's own privacy policy.

What Puck Haul does not add. As of November 5, 2026, Puck Haul has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking scripts.

Links to NHL.com and other sites follow those sites' own policies.
